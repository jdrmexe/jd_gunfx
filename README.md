# JD Gun Effects & RPG FX (`jd_gunfx` & `jd_rpgsfx`)

A comprehensive suite of audio-visual enhancements for firearms and heavy weaponry in FiveM. This resource introduces immersive soundscapes, custom HTML-based audio triggers, bullet whiz-by effects, and RPG explosion/launch audio mechanics.

---

## 📁 Included Resources

* **`jd_gunfx`**: Adds weapon sound design elements, audio feedback, and dynamic bullet whiz-by sounds when rounds fly past the player.
* **`jd_rpgsfx`**: Dedicated sound and visual effect handler for RPGs and heavy projectile launchers.

---

## ⚙️ Installation & Setup

1. Place both `jd_gunfx` and `jd_rpgsfx` directories into your FiveM server's `resources` folder.
2. Add the following lines to your `server.cfg` configuration file:

```cfg
ensure jd_gunfx
ensure jd_rpgsfx
